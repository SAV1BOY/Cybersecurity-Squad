# Security Awareness Programs — Exemplary Models

## Purpose
Curated examples of security awareness programs that actually change behavior, not just check compliance boxes.

## Gold-Standard Programs

### 1. Phishing Simulation Campaigns
- **Graduated difficulty**: Start with obvious phishing, escalate to spear-phishing over months
- **Positive reinforcement**: Reward reporters, don't punish clickers (first offense)
- **Real metrics**: Click rate, report rate, time-to-report, repeat offender rate
- **Contextual training**: Deliver micro-training within 60 seconds of a click

### 2. Security Champion Programs
- **Embedded champions**: One per engineering team, 10-20% time allocation
- **Skill ladder**: Awareness → Practitioner → Champion → Advocate
- **Incentives**: Conference attendance, certification funding, recognition
- **Deliverables**: Monthly threat briefing, quarterly code review sessions

### 3. Gamified Security Training
- **CTF for non-security staff**: Simplified challenges teaching real concepts
- **Leaderboards**: Team-based competition, not individual shaming
- **Scenario-based**: "You received this email, what do you do?"
- **Frequency**: Monthly micro-challenges (5-10 min), quarterly deep dives (1 hour)

### 4. Executive Security Briefings
- **Board-level**: Quarterly risk posture, incident trends, benchmark vs industry
- **C-suite**: Monthly threat landscape, active campaigns targeting sector
- **VP-level**: Bi-weekly security metrics relevant to their domain
- **Format**: 10-slide max, risk quantified in business terms ($$, reputation, regulatory)

## Metrics That Matter

| Metric | Target | Measurement |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| Phishing report rate | >60% | Reports / total phishing simulations |
| Mean time to report | <5 min | Time from delivery to report |
| Repeat clicker rate | <5% | Users clicking 2+ simulations |
| Security ticket volume | Increasing | Voluntary security reports from staff |
| Champion coverage | >80% teams | Teams with active champion / total teams |
| Training completion | >95% | Completed within SLA / total assigned |

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid
- Shaming employees publicly for security failures
- Annual compliance-only training (checkbox exercises)
- Generic content not tailored to role or department
- No measurement of behavioral change
- Punitive-first culture that discourages reporting
