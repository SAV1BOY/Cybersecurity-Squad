## Usage Notes

- Deliver quarterly to executive leadership and board
- Keep to 3-5 pages maximum for executive consumption
- Use visual risk indicators (red/yellow/green) in presented versions
- Prepare talking points for verbal presentation
- Archive for year-over-year trend analysis
- Customize risk language to organizational risk appetite
